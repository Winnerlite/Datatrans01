How do I check current intercity bus prices in Nigeria? +

Select your departure state and destination above to compare 2026 fare estimates across 43+ Nigerian bus companies, view departure terminals, or join our WhatsApp VIP group for live park updates.

Which transport companies have the cheapest interstate fares? +

Standard Hiace and Sienna operators such as Peace Mass Transit, Benue Links, Plateau Riders, and state-backed transport services typically offer lower base fares than executive coaches.

Do Nigerian interstate buses charge extra for heavy Ghana-must-go bags? +

Yes. Most Hiace and Sienna operators allow one standard cabin bag free, while large Ghana-must-go sacks or commercial cartons attract a luggage surcharge at the loading bay. Check operator rules or ask commuters in our WhatsApp VIP group before arriving at the park.

Can I drop off at a highway junction before the final motor park? +

Yes, major interstate buses drop passengers at recognised expressway junctions along the route—such as Ore, Lokoja, 9th Mile, or Gwagwalada—though you still pay the full terminal-to-terminal fare. Compare official park terminals on our Nigerian cities directory.

Why is the online bus ticket price different from the morning park counter? +

App bookings include gateway service fees and fixed seat tiers, while physical motor parks adjust prices dynamically based on morning passenger queues and fuel demand. Use our route search above to compare baseline fares across 43+ operators before paying.

How do I verify if a Nigerian transport agent phone number is genuine? +

Never transfer ticket money to personal bank or OPay accounts found on random social media comments. Cross-check official terminal numbers on our operator pages or verify agents inside the Intercity Prices VIP group.
