Nigeria Bus Prices: What to Know Before You Travel

Intercity bus fares in Nigeria are not fixed. The price you pay depends on the transport company, the park you leave from, the type of bus, the day you travel and the season. This guide explains what moves the price and how to check a fare before you leave home, whether you use our WhatsApp group or not.

Why bus fares differ so much between parks

  • Transport company: Large operators and smaller park-based companies price the same route differently.
  • Terminal: A trip that starts at one Lagos park can cost more or less than the same trip from another.
  • Bus type: Air-conditioned buses, 7-seaters and executive vehicles usually cost more than standard buses.
  • Day of travel: Fares often rise on weekends and Fridays and can be lower in the middle of the week.
  • Season and fuel cost: Christmas, Easter and other holidays push prices up, and so do fuel price changes.

Terminal-to-terminal prices vs city-to-city prices

Most price lists only say "Lagos to Abuja". But the fare you actually pay depends on where you board and where you get off. Jibowu to Utako is not always the same price as Ojota to Jabi. That is why the VIP group works on terminal-to-terminal prices: you name your starting park and your destination park, and you get an answer for that exact trip.

How to check a fare before you leave home

  1. Search your route on the Intercity Prices homepage to see which companies run it and their price ranges.
  2. Compare at least two or three companies instead of taking the first price you hear.
  3. Ask in the VIP group with your route, both terminals and your travel date to get a current answer from admins and members.
  4. Confirm the price at the terminal, pay at the company's counter, and keep your ticket or payment receipt.

How to avoid fake agents and park touts

  • Do not pay strangers who approach you outside the terminal, even if they wear a company vest.
  • Be careful with prices far below what other companies charge for the same route.
  • Check that the company name on your ticket matches the bus you are boarding.
  • Take a photo of your ticket and the bus before you travel.
  • If you are unsure about an agent, ask in the group before you pay. Other members may already know them.
